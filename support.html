Getting started

How do I add a recipe?

Home, Import a recipe. Photograph a recipe card or a cookbook page, choose a PDF, paste text or a link, or share a page from Safari to ReciPen. Photograph every page. The pages are sent to ReciPen's AI to read them and are not stored; the result is a tidy, searchable recipe you check on the Review screen before saving. Without AI credits, the text your phone reads from the page is used instead.

What does the AI do?

It tidies imported text into a recipe, writes a recipe with you in Create a recipe, answers questions about a saved recipe in Ask about this recipe (swap what you don't have, change it for a crowd), scales servings and estimates nutrition. Everything else in the app works without it and without a connection.

How do I share a recipe?

Open the recipe and tap the share icon. ReciPen makes a link that carries the recipe itself: anyone with ReciPen opens it straight into the app, anyone else sees it as a web page. A very long recipe is shared as text instead. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Do I need an account?

No. Your recipes live on your phone. Use Settings, Backup to save a copy to Files or move to a new phone.

Credits and ReciPen Plus

What is a credit?

Every AI action costs one credit: an import with AI cleanup, each message in Ask or Create, a scaling, or a nutrition estimate. A new install starts with 25 credits and gets 5 more on the first of every month. Capturing, typing, editing, searching, tagging and backing up never cost a credit.

What does Plus include?

Unlimited AI, for $2.99 a month, with a fair-use limit far beyond everyday cooking. Everything you have saved stays yours whether or not you subscribe.

How do I cancel, or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions. Refunds are requested from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play; they decide, and we cannot override them.

I bought Plus but the app still says Free

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. If it is still not recognised after a minute, email us the Diagnostics block (Settings, Help, Diagnostics) and the purchase receipt email from Apple or Google.

I have a code

Settings, Plan, Have a code? Enter it and apply.

Troubleshooting

"AI limit reached" or "Out of AI credits"

You have used this month's credits. More arrive on the first of the month, or Plus removes the limit. You can still capture the recipe with basic on-device extraction; it may need more tidying by hand.

"Needs a connection"

The AI features need the internet. Everything else works offline.

The photo didn't read well

Fill the frame with the page, avoid glare, keep the text straight, and photograph every page of a long recipe. Handwriting and ornate fonts usually read fine; if something comes out wrong, the Review screen lets you fix it before saving.

My dish photo disappeared

Update to the latest version; an earlier build stored photos in a way that an app update could lose. Photos added since the fix survive updates. Restore from a backup if you have one.

Something else is wrong

Email us what you were doing, with the Diagnostics block from the app (Settings, Help, Diagnostics, tap to copy): it carries your Support ID, the app version, your phone's system version and the last error the AI returned. A screenshot helps.
